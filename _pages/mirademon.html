MIRADEMON

A creature born from years of collective experimentation.

Mirademon is the new incarnation of an experiment that began years ago around a shared table, with soldering irons, LEDs, wires, people and shared knowledge.

Born from years of hands-on electronics workshops, Mirademon transforms Lutòpia's experiments with light, sensors, open hardware and collective making into a living kinetic installation.

From the workshop table to the creature

For years, Lutòpia has gathered around a shared table to solder LEDs, build electronic jewellery, experiment with wearable technology, explore autonomous communication systems and share tools, knowledge and ideas.

Mirademon grows out of that same table.

What began as a collection of small personal electronic objects gradually evolved into something larger: a shared artificial creature capable of seeing, moving, lighting up and reacting to the people around it.

Mirademon 2026

Mirademon is an interactive kinetic and animatronic sculpture created as part of Lutòpia's ongoing experimentation.

A Seeed Studio reCamera acts as its digital eye, using a standard computer-vision model to perceive the surrounding environment.

A Seeed Studio reTerminal coordinates the creature's behaviour through Node-RED, Python and Flask, controlling its eyes, jaw, LEDs, sound and servo mechanisms.

It watches. It moves. It reacts. It becomes aware of the presence of the people around it.

The workshop: where Mirademon comes from

Mirademon is not an isolated artwork. It is part of a longer Lutòpia practice developed through years of peer-to-peer workshops.

Around a shared table, participants have created LED decorations, electronic jewellery, wearable devices, communication tools and small interactive objects.

The workshop is not based on passive teaching. People learn by making, helping each other, experimenting and occasionally breaking things.

Mirademon takes this same philosophy and scales it up into a collective artificial creature.

The workshop became a creature.

A living workshop

Mirademon can become both installation and workshop platform.

Participants can discover how its eyes move, how sensors perceive the environment, how software generates behaviour and how light, sound and physical movement can give an object something resembling personality.

The creature is therefore never entirely finished. It can be observed, modified, discussed and extended.
